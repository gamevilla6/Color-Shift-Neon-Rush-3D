# Store name and listing readiness review

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-22  
**Scope:** Preliminary public search and review of the project’s existing store-facing metadata for a planned Google Play release. Target countries were not specified, so this is not a territory-specific clearance.

## Findings

### The game name is inconsistent

- The artifact metadata calls the game **Color Swipe Runner** and describes a neon 3D arcade runner.
- The game page’s HTML title, description, and visible branding call it **Color Shift: Neon Rush 3D**.
- These names are not interchangeable. Choose one final name and use it consistently in the app, artifact metadata, Play listing, screenshots, and promotional graphics before submission.

### Preliminary title search

Public web and store searches for the exact and similar names surfaced:

- **Color Swipe Runner:** no exact game title was surfaced in the searches. Similar mobile-game names include [Color Swipe 3D – Match Puzzle on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.refreshinggames.swipeblockpainting3d) and [Color Runner – Color Rush on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/color-runner-color-rush/id1407608207).
- **Color Shift: Neon Rush 3D:** searches surfaced [Neon Rush 3D: Endless Runner on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.my.neonrush), as well as other games using “Neon Rush.” “Color Shift” also appears to be a common, less distinctive phrase.

These are potential discoverability or confusion signals, not a legal finding that any title infringes another mark. A public web search is not a trademark clearance search.

Official sources to search once the final name and release territories are set:

- [USPTO Trademark Search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/) (United States)
- [UK IPO keyword/phrase search](https://trademarks.ipo.gov.uk/ipo-tmtext/start) (United Kingdom)
- [EUIPO TMview](https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/) (European Union and participating offices)
- [WIPO Global Brand Database](https://branddb.wipo.int/en/quicksearch) (international and participating national collections)

Search the exact wording and similar spellings/sounds, and review relevant goods and services (including games/software). The EUIPO notes that a database search can reduce, but does not eliminate, risk. Obtain qualified trademark advice before committing to a name or filing.

## Google Play metadata review

Google Play’s official [Metadata policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9898842?hl=en) requires honest, relevant metadata; it limits app titles to 30 characters and prohibits misleading, repetitive, or inappropriate metadata. The [Intellectual Property policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9888072?hl=en) requires rights to the content and marks used.

- The current page title, “Color Shift: Neon Rush 3D” (25 characters), and artifact title, “Color Swipe Runner” (18 characters), are each within the 30-character title limit. The mismatch still needs to be resolved.
- The page meta description says “build your combo.” Confirm that a combo mechanic is present and accurately represented before reusing that claim in the listing.
- The artifact description includes keyboard and touch controls. Confirm the Android build supports the described controls and features; describe the Android release, not only the current web version.
- No Play short description, full listing description, app icon, feature graphic, or store screenshots were found in the project. These materials are not ready for policy review. Google requires an app icon, a short description of no more than 80 characters, and a feature graphic for a published listing; its [preview-asset guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151?hl=en) also requires assets to accurately showcase the app.
- No prepared store images or external-font/image/audio assets were found. Once screenshots are made, ensure they show actual gameplay and do not contain unsupported claims, third-party branding, ranking or promotional badges, or other misleading elements.
